# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) (full text at that link, which applies here in full).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, keep feedback about the work and not the person, and do not harass anyone. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues and contributors that violate this.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through GitHub: use the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form and mark the report as a conduct issue, or contact the maintainer listed on the repository profile. A dedicated contact address will be published here once one exists.
